Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns for you.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and font services, to operate the Service. These providers only process the minimum data necessary to deliver their function and do not have access to your expense records.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details listed on your account settings page.
